Inside the Atelier

Built by eleven hands, signed by one

Our Copenhagen workshop keeps a deliberate ceiling of forty commissions per year. Every piece carries the mark of the lead maker who saw it from rough plank to final oiling. Visits are by appointment, and we encourage them. Furniture should be met in person before it is chosen.
